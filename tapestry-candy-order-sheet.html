Do these in this order

  1. Order the $7.99 sample kit below. Eight pouches across four sizes, free shipping.
  2. Freeze-dry a batch, fill one of each size, weigh them. Now you know both the size and the net weight, and neither is a guess.
  3. Order the winning size in bulk, plus candy, desiccant and the sealer.
  4. Send printers a quote request for the label sizes that suit the bag you picked.
  5. If a shop wants to scan it, buy a GTIN and the barcode regenerates from it. Skip otherwise.

Desiccant

Silica gel, not oxygen absorbers. Candy fails by picking up moisture and going tacky, and O2 absorbers do nothing about that. Tyvek so it can't shed dust into the product.

Sealer

Seal width is the spec that matters, not length. A 5 mm band holds on 7-mil foil laminate; the narrow 1.6 mm units are built for thin poly and can channel. Bought once.
