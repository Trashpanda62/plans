Sales curve

Gamalytic's public estimates for 1,443 Indie-class Steam games priced $13.99 to $15.99, released 2022 to 2024. The top 25% starts at 12,290 copies.

Price per copy

Launch copies sell at list minus the launch discount. Later copies sell at the realized share of list (about 70% for premium tower defense, from our market dossier) because of sales.

Assumptions

Console and mobile unit shares, the launch share, and both cosmetic rates have no source. Console defaults keep console revenue near 30%, 10% and 20% of Steam's at the higher console price.

Not in the model

Price elasticity, refunds, sales tax and VAT, regional pricing, DLC, a porting partner's share, and the 15% small-business store rate unless you set it.
