Before this goes to print

Net weight reads 3 OZ (85 g) as a placeholder. Fill one pouch, weigh it, and the real figure replaces it. The barcode is a dummy until the GS1 number exists.

Every colour in the rainbow wordmark clears a 3:1 contrast ratio against the cream field. The obvious bright rainbow does not — its yellow lands at 1.7:1 and prints as a hole in the word.
